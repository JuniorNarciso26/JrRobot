JrBot — HW04 Assembly Confirmation

Confirmed by the project owner:
- Controller board: ESP32-S3-N16R8, two USB-C connectors.
- Camera: OV5640 on the board's camera connector.
- OLED: SSD1306 I2C 128x64; SDA=GPIO1, SCL=GPIO2; it may be disconnected in the current assembly.
- Microphone: MS3625 I2S.
- Amplifier: MAX98357A mono I2S.
- Speaker: 4 ohms / 3 W.

Defined audio pin mapping:
- GPIO21 -> microphone SCK/BCLK and amplifier BCLK.
- GPIO47 -> microphone WS and amplifier LRC/WS.
- GPIO41 <- microphone SD.
- GPIO42 -> amplifier DIN.

Before applying power, check continuity, VDD/VIN/GND and absence of shorts with the board powered off.
